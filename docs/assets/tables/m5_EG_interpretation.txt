Cointegration between the quantiles of lnCO2 and lnGDP is found for 6 of 121 quantile pairs (5%). It is most frequent at low quantiles of lnCO2 (6% of pairs with tau <= 0.2) versus high quantiles (3% with tau >= 0.8). Among cointegrated pairs, the long-run coefficient ranges from 0.692 to 1.101 (median 0.782); 6 are significant at 5%. The largest significant long-run effect (1.101) arises when lnGDP is at its 0.05 quantile and lnCO2 at its 0.10 quantile: a 1% rise in lnGDP is associated with a 1.10% change in lnCO2 in the long run (log-log specification). The error-correction term is negative and significant for 104 of 121 pairs (median -0.365: about 37% of a deviation from equilibrium is corrected each period). Short-run effects are significant for 94 pairs, with median 0.231.
