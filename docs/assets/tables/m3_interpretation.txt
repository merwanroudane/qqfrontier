Over the quantile range [0.05, 0.95], the expLM test rejects the joint null of no and stable Granger causality from US2Y to US2Y10Y in quantiles (p = 0.012); supLM gives p = 0.080. The conventional supWald test, which ignores instability, gives p = 0.000. Causality is detected in the ranges [0.40, 0.60], [0.80, 0.95]. Quantile by quantile, LM(tau) is significant at 5% for 42 of 91 levels (from 0.22 to 0.95). The instability component (sup LM1) dominates the causality component (LM2) at 5% of the quantiles, so the rejection is driven mainly by a stable non-zero effect.
