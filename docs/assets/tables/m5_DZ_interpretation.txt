Cointegration between the quantiles of lnCO2 and lnGDP is found for 53 of 121 quantile pairs (44%). It is most frequent at low quantiles of lnCO2 (88% of pairs with tau <= 0.2) versus high quantiles (12% with tau >= 0.8). Among cointegrated pairs, the long-run coefficient ranges from -1.833 to 1.531 (median 0.582); 36 are significant at 5%. The largest significant long-run effect (1.531) arises when lnGDP is at its 0.95 quantile and lnCO2 at its 0.95 quantile: a 1% rise in lnGDP is associated with a 1.53% change in lnCO2 in the long run (log-log specification). The error-correction term is negative and significant for 115 of 121 pairs (median -0.447: about 45% of a deviation from equilibrium is corrected each period). Short-run effects are significant for 76 pairs, with median 0.393.
